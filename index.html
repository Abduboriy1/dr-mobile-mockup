FAQ

Good questions.

Anything else? We answer texts fast.

Do you need water or power at my location?

We arrive fully equipped to work at your home, office or garage. If anything specific is needed for your location, we’ll confirm it when booking.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Most coating packages take one to two days depending on how much paint correction is needed. Coatings also need time to cure, so we’ll plan the appointment around your schedule.

Can you work on wrapped or PPF-protected cars?

Yes. Film and vinyl get film-safe chemistry and dedicated PPF coatings — we work on filmed, satin-wrapped and stealth vehicles regularly.

What’s the difference between enhancement and correction?

An enhancement is a single-stage polish that boosts gloss and removes light swirls. Correction is multi-stage and targets deeper defects for a near-flawless finish.

How do I maintain a coated car?

Regular pH-neutral washes keep a coating performing. Our Maintenance and Concierge plans handle that for you with coating-safe products and inspections.

Do you require a deposit?

We’re appointment-only. A small deposit secures your date and goes toward your final invoice.
